## 実際に送信したプロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production art-direction board for modular very-low-poly 3D scenery assets for the Japanese delivery driver app ハコ虎.
Create one landscape landscape asset sheet, beautifully arranged in a clean evenly spaced four-row grid on pale blue-grey. No phones or app UI. Models should be plausible easy-to-build mesh assets, not illustrations pasted on planes. All assets have an identical three-quarter orthographic camera angle, neutral soft daylight, subtle contact shadows and a visible flat-shaded extremely low-poly style. Restrained muted sage green, muted teal green, chalk white, mist grey, muted blue, medium warm wood. The scenery must stay understated so a silver kei van can remain the hero.
Row 1 label 「木」: four separate specimens: squat broadleaf tree with one faceted crown and simple hexagonal trunk, taller narrow faceted broadleaf tree, a simple three-tier angular conifer, a very small faceted shrub. Clear silhouette with only a handful of polygon planes. No individual leaves.
Row 2 label 「建物」: three individual minimalist rectangular blocks of different heights, one low apartment block, one simple warehouse with a small grey roof. Just solid pastel cuboids, with at most 2-3 large window bands as flat material rectangles, no detailed facades, no signage, no brick texture.
Row 3 label 「雲」: three distinct white angular cluster-cloud silhouettes, each made of 3-5 faceted chunky volumes. Not fluffy realistic clouds, no spheres with hundreds of subdivisions. Include one very long thin low cloud variant.
Row 4 label 「地面・小物」: one short straight road TILE with a broken white line that can repeat seamlessly, one simple sidewalk TILE, a 2-plank park bench with 4 legs, and a very soft oval contact-shadow decal sample.
Add a tiny scale-reference generic silver Japanese KEI CARGO VAN at the lower right margin, not a truck, no recognizable corporate logo. Show a single small assembled scene vignette at the upper right only if room, otherwise omit it.
Title 「ハコ虎 背景素材案」 at top. Under title short subtitle 「木・建物・雲を組み替える」. NO triangle numbers or performance claims: this is an art-direction concept sheet, not certified geometry. No bevel-heavy toy plastic, no tiny detail, no neon, no emoji, no photorealistic textures, no watermark. Readable Japanese sans-serif labels. Aim for the low-poly background of the provided reference: quiet stage scenery, not the main subject.
